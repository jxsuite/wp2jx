# Fidelity log

One section per round of the fidelity loop on the pilot (finelinepainting, `wp2jx verify` against https://finelinepainting.pro). Numbers are the verifier's: the whole sitemap (141 pages compared of 144 listed; 2 are not served by the migrated site, 1 cannot be captured because the live page redirects to itself), both viewports (1366 and 390 px), pixel tolerance 0.1, the live captures cached in `.dev/verify-pilot-cache` so that a run re-captures only the migrated pages. A page's fidelity is the mean of its two viewports; the mean and median below are over pages unless a viewport is named.

### How the causes were found

A pixel match is a position match: a block that sits 26 px lower than on the live page costs everything below it. So the evidence that mattered was not the diff images but where the layout first moves (`layout.first-shift`, `layout.size-changed`), then a probe that captures the live and the migrated page the way `verify` does and lists, top to bottom, the elements that carry the same Cwicly classID on both sides with their boxes and computed styles (scratch tools, `.dev/r1/diffrects.ts` and friends; not part of the repository). Every difference below was reduced to one element whose box or style differs, then to the markup or rule that makes it so.

### Fixes

Each was measured on a sample of 12 to 19 pages after a rebuild (the whole run takes about an hour), and every fix has a unit test over the real fixtures, hand-mutated to check the test fails without it. Effects are page fidelity, before to after, for the pages the fix is about; they are not additive (a page often has several causes).

1. **A font a block stylesheet imports was never loaded** (`src/site.ts`, `src/emit/project.ts`). Cwicly writes `@import url("https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Reem Kufi…")` at the head of the stylesheet of a template, post or component whose blocks pick a Google font. A style object cannot hold a statement at-rule, so the import went into a page's `style` as an empty rule and vanished; only the global classes' imports reached the head. Subjects now record the `@import`s of their stylesheets (and the ones blocks hoist) per site, and the assembler links each once from the project's `$head`. Effect: the top bar's and main nav's `Reem Kufi` text now has the live width (`layout.size-changed` of `.navitems`/`.navlink` on 101 pages is gone), a few pixels on every page rather than a layout shift. Also brings in `Inter`.
2. **Fluent Forms were an `[fluentform]` placeholder** (`src/wp/fluentform.ts`, `src/emit/fluentform.ts`, resolvers in pages, templates and components, `src/emit/project.ts`). The form of a service page's hero (22 pages), `/quote/`, `/contact-us/` and the line painting hero were literal text, and the hero's missing height moved everything below it. The forms are now read from the plugin's tables (`fluentform_forms`, the styler's `ffs_*` meta) and drawn with the plugin's own markup (the class and tag sequence matches the live HTML of forms 1, 3, 5 and 6 exactly), styled by the plugin's two public stylesheets plus each form's styler rules, shipped as `public/css/fluentform.css` and linked once. A form cannot be submitted (no backend): reported as `form.not-submittable`. Effect: `/quote/` 36.4 to 85.9, `/contact-us/` 54.9 to 75.1, `/service/log-homes/` 49.7 to 58.1, `/service/line-painting/` 43.1 to 47.0.
3. **The bot check of those forms** (`src/emit/fluentform.ts`, `src/emit/project.ts`). The plugin prints `<div class="cf-turnstile" data-sitekey=…>` under the submit button and Cloudflare's script fills it with a 65 px widget; without it the hero card was 103 px shorter than live and every service page sat 26 px high all the way down. The widget is written the way the plugin writes it, from the plugin's own options (`cfturnstile_*`; the secret key is never read), with Cloudflare's script in the head. The key is bound to the site's domain, so on `127.0.0.1` the widget shows its domain error (the verifier counts these as `request.failed`/`console.error` on 21 pages: an artifact of the check, not of the site). Effect at 1366: `/quote/` 85.9 to 95.7, `/service/line-painting/` 47.7 to 54.7, `/service/log-homes/` 57.8 to 69.7; at 390 the same pages move the other way (their hero carries a paragraph the live template no longer has, see below), so the mean over both viewports moves little (81.8 to 82.0).
4. **A featured image's size** (`src/cwicly/dynamic.ts`, `src/cwicly/blocks/layout.ts`). On a static conversion (the related-posts cards written into 31 project entries) the image was printed without the `width` and `height` WordPress prints for the size the block asks for (`medium_large`, 768 px) because the saved tag has none; the build then stamped the original's, and `width:auto; max-height:10rem` made each card 1,280 px wide. It is printed with the size's own dimensions now. Effect: the section was 1,224 px too tall on 31 pages; `/project/garage-cracked-walls/` 74.8 to 84.8, `/project/log-cabin-staining-in-fredericksburg-pa/` 78.1 to 89.1.
5. **An image block that names no file left no element** (`src/cwicly/blocks/layout.ts`). The plugin prints `<img class="image-cf6348e …"/>` with no `src`; in the Icon Card's flex column it still takes its place and the row gap (16 px) sits around it, so every card heading was 16 px high (51 page-viewports had it as their first layout shift). Now printed (with an empty `alt`). An old test listed this as a known difference of another module. Effect: `/premium-paint/` 90.6 to 100.0.
6. **A condition on the address of the page was dropped** (`src/cwicly/conditions.ts`). The site's own PHP helper `get_current_slug()` (the last segment of the request path) hid the Pennsylvania map on every other service area and the area photo on Pennsylvania; both showed everywhere (a 30 px neutral `[display-map]` line above every county's photo). It is read as the archive term's slug, the entry's slug or the address of the page, and becomes a binding or is decided. Effect: `/service_area/lebanon-county-pa/` 59.6 to 64.9 and the first-shift of 17 pages is gone.
7. **A shortcode block's markup went through no `wpautop`** (`src/core/blocks.ts`). WordPress prints a lone `<script>` in a shortcode block as `<p><script …></script></p>` (checked on the live pages); the paragraph's 16 px margins are 32 px of the Trustindex container's height, so every service page was 32 px short from there down. Effect: `/service/log-homes/` 58.1 to 65.3.
8. **The `sizes` and size of a component's image** (`src/cwicly/dynamic.ts`, `src/cwicly/blocks/layout.ts`). The Image Card's picture is printed by the plugin with its width, height and `sizes="auto, (max-width: Wpx) 100vw, Wpx"`; the build wrote none of them, and a lazy image with `sizes=auto` is a different intrinsic size to a flex container (77 px against 160 px; measured by changing the attributes in the live DOM). The component now binds them to the instance's own image. A first attempt that wrote `auto` on every image broke the 50-year badge (an eager image there) and was removed.
9. **The before and after slider was dropped** (`src/core/image-compare.ts`, registered in `src/core/blocks.ts`; stylesheet shipped as `public/css/image-compare.css`). `icb/image-compare` saves no markup, so it was a `wp2jx-block` placeholder, a blank in the five project entries that hold one (Markdown cannot hold a placeholder) and a 0 px container in the services. The slider is drawn at rest, as the plugin's script leaves it before anyone drags (both images, the second clipped to the right half, a handle in the middle), with the plugin's `view.css` and an `aspect-ratio` instead of the script's pixel height. Effect: `/project/barn-painting-in-lebanon/` 60.0 to 89.5, `/project/wedding-venue-barn-staining-in-york-pa/` 57.9 to 86.1, `/project/barn-painting-in-manheim-pa/` 61.1 to 86.6, `/service/kitchen-cabinet-painters/` 52.9 to 68.1.

### Remaining differences, and what they are

In the order of what they cost.

1. **Pagination of query lists** (`/projects/` 22.6, `/blog/` 61.4, `/category/blog/` 61.4). The live pages show the first 6 projects or 5 posts and a "load more" device; the migrated pages list all 82 or 11 (a decision recorded in `src/cwicly/blocks/data.ts`: a list with no way to reach the rest would lose content, reported as `query.pagination`). On a pixel comparison this is the whole difference. A middle way that keeps the content and the live look is a no-JavaScript load-more (a checkbox and a label, the entries past the page size hidden until it is checked); it needs the page size carried to the emitter and is the one change here that is a decision for the site's owner.
2. **The dump is older than the live site.** The largest group of what is left. Confirmed on the live HTML: `/about-us/` has a "Meet Our Team" section the database lacks; the live `single-service` template dropped the hero's "Professional interior painting done right…" paragraph (140 px at 390 px, it is the reason fix 3 reads as a loss at 390); the live `single-project` template ends with an image and "Call us today…" call to action the database's template does not have (`text.missing` on all 82 project pages); some lists have a row more on the live site (24 projects against 22); a few images are other files. None of it is a converter defect; a fresh export is the cure, and the verifier would then say what is real.
3. **JavaScript widgets** (accepted): the Interactive Geo Map on `/service_area/pennsylvania/` (765 px, 41.1), the Trustindex widget (masked on both sides), the chat widget, the form's submission.
4. **Two redirects kept as pages** (`/hardwood-floor-refinishing/` 43.3, one more in `project_tag`): Jx writes a redirect page where a page of the same address would be, so `src/emit/redirects.ts` keeps the page and drops the Rank Math rule (`redirect.shadowed`, a recorded decision). On the live site the rule wins.
5. **Rules of one class from different posts meet in the project style.** Entries cannot hold style blocks, so the rules their stylesheets hoist are written to `project.json` and apply to every page that uses the class: `.heading-c235f2d { @--sm { font-size: 20px } }` from the project entries reaches the blog's cards at 390 px (28 elements on 6 pages, `style.systematic`). Scoping them to the entry pages is the fix and a larger change.
6. **Core block CSS after the template's.** On the live pages the block library's inline CSS is printed last; here a page's own style (`.content-post h2 { padding: 2rem }`) comes after the project-level core rules, so a heading with a background has 32 px padding and not the library's `1.25em 2.375em` (`style.systematic`, blog posts). Reordering the project-level layers does not reach it (the template's rule is page-level); it needs the core rules to be raised in specificity or placed in a later sheet.
7. **Entry-mode images do not carry sizes.** The bound `<img>` of a card in a collection template has the original's dimensions (the entry's data holds no size table); a page that sets `width:auto` there is wider than live. The static case is fixed (fix 4).

### How the causes were found

Round 1's method, one level down. Where `layout.first-shift` names an element that sits on both pages, a probe (scratch tools under `.dev/r2/`, not part of the repository) captures the live page and the migrated one the way `verify` does and prints that element's box and computed style on each side, then lists the CSS rules of the migrated page that match it and set the property (`document.styleSheets`, media queries evaluated). Every fix below started as a property with two values and ended as one rule that is in the wrong place, the wrong specificity, the wrong order or missing.

One thing the probes made plain, and which bounds what is left: the dump is older than the live site (the database was exported on 2026-09-16 and the live Cwicly stylesheets were regenerated on the 17th), and a page that differs by a block is not comparable below that block. Where the live page and the migrated one print the same element under different classes (`heading-c1ffff1` against `heading-cf07cdc`), the difference is the dump's age, and no rule of ours can be right about it.

### Fixes

Measured on samples of 12 to 63 pages after each rebuild (the whole run is 30 minutes), then on the whole sitemap. Every fix has unit tests over the real fixtures; each was hand-mutated in a scratch copy of `src/` and `tests/` under `.dev/mut-r2/` (26 mutants; four survived the first tests and each got a test that kills it: the order of media blocks, a site with no item paragraph to take out, the address in a rule a converter hoisted, an empty image slot with no twin on the live page).

1. **An entry's rule for a class that the pages style otherwise** (`src/emit/collections.ts`, `src/emit/class-styles.ts`, wired in `src/emit/project.ts`). The live page prints the template's stylesheet and then the post's, so on a project page the post's rule wins every property it sets. A Jx page orders the same two rules by where their elements are in the tree, and the template's element that comes after the body won (a related-project card 9 px too tall: `padding: 5px 5px 14px` of the template's `.div-c3a6482` over the post's `5px`; an image 175 px high where the post says `auto`: 83 project pages had a `layout.first-shift` of 10 px at the first card, and everything below it moved). Worse, a rule that an entry moved to the project's `style` (a heading's, a custom element's) reached every page that uses the class: `.heading-c235f2d { @--sm { font-size: 20px; padding: 0 } }` of the projects restyled the blog's cards, and `.heading-c90571a { margin-bottom: 30px }` pushed `/commercial/` 30 px down. Now `buildCollections` is given what the pages, layouts and components style, class by class (`collectClassStyles`: the Jx files, and the items of a query loop, which are the text of an expression and are read by a quote-aware scanner and `JSON.parse`, never evaluated). An entry's style for such a class is cut in two: what the page's rule contradicts (the same property at another value, a shorthand against a longhand, a small-screen override of a base declaration) is written for `.class.jx-<hash>`, one class more specific, with the media blocks of the same property and their order; the rest stays where the build writes it, on the page's own style, or, for a rule that already moved to the project, for `.class:where(.jx-<hash>)`, which only this entry's elements carry. A class that another element of the same body carries as a second class (`columns-c474385 featured-columns`) is shared, and its rule keeps reaching all of them: the first version of this fix isolated it and `/service/log-homes/` fell from 60.8 to 48.7 (the grid its sections sit in lost its rule). Effect on 16 projects: 87.2 to 87.8, `style.mismatch` pages 120 to 42, `/commercial/` 94.5 to 99.5, the 83 first-shifts of the cards gone.
2. **A text field's markup is printed as markup** (`src/cwicly/tokens.ts`). The pilot's Cwicly (1.4.7; the repository's copy, 1.6.0, escapes with `esc_html`, which is what this tool had modelled) returns an ACF text or textarea value as it is stored, and the live pages show a link typed into the field as a link. The entry data had `<a href=”…”>` (the text turned by `wptexturize` tag and all, a broken tag) and the element printed it as text. Now `printsAcfRaw(ctx)` (the data format's version before 1.6.0) makes those two field types HTML: the binding is an `innerHTML`, the static literal is markup, and the value is turned by `texturizeHtml`, which leaves the tags alone. A site of a newer plugin, or one that does not say, keeps the escaping. `/service_area/berks-county-pa/` 63.9 to 81.0, `lancaster` 66.2 to 82.0, `chester` 73.5 to 84.7; the 17 service areas 75.5 to 77.8.
3. **The paragraph a list item or table cell is written with** (`src/emit/collections.ts`). The converter and the Markdown reader put a `<p>` around the text of every list item (`md.item-paragraphs`), and the theme's `p { margin: 1rem 0 }` made every item of every list in a post 8 px taller; the live pages have no paragraph there (0 of `<li><p` on all 8 posts with lists). The project's style now carries `li > p, td > p, th > p, dd > p { margin: 0 !important; padding: 0 !important }` for the item tags that no saved post, page or template of the site holds a paragraph in (`collection.item-paragraph-rule`; `!important` because the theme's `.content-post p` is more specific than a tag). `/7-common-questions-about-professional-painters/` 72.5 to 88.2, `/the-benefits-of-premium-paint/` 79.4 to 94.4, `/5-steps-to-staining-a-log-cabin/` 76.2 to 85.9, the 12 blog and project pages of the sample 82.5 to 87.4.
4. **A table the author fixed keeps its columns** (`src/emit/collections.ts`). A Markdown table has no class, so the figure carries `has-fixed-layout`, and WordPress's rule is written for `.wp-block-table .has-fixed-layout` (the table inside): it matched nothing, and the columns sized to their content (the report already said so). The project's style carries the rule for the figure (`collection.fixed-layout-rule`). `/why-are-barns-painted-red/` 92.6 to 96.5, `/guide-to-the-wonders-of-spraying-doors/` 91.6 to 96.5, `/5-steps-to-staining-a-log-cabin/` 85.9 to 89.5, `/7-common-questions-about-professional-painters/` 88.2 to 91.2; the 12 pages of the sample 87.4 to 89.2.
5. **Media blocks of hoisted rules come after the design system's** (`src/emit/project.ts`, `liftMediaBlocks`). Jx writes every selector entry of the project style and only then the top-level `@--md` and `@--sm` blocks, so a hoisted `.card { @--sm { … } }` came before the design system's `@--md` block and lost to it below 576 px, where the narrower query has to win (`.gallery-c1bef94 .cc-gallery { repeat(3) }` at 576 px lost to `.gallery-default .cc-gallery { repeat(2) }` at 992 px: a gallery of three columns showed two, `image.rendered-size` on 24 page-viewports). The media blocks of the selector entries move into the project's own blocks, which follow `$media` in order. `/project/painting-newly-built-house-in-lebanon-pa/` 83.4 to 89.0; `image.rendered-size` pages 18 to 15 (the others are the dump's galleries).
6. **A query with a "Load more" button is its first page and the button** (`src/cwicly/blocks/data.ts`, `pagedByClick`, `withLoadMore`). The plugin renders the first page in the browser (`frontendRendering`) and fetches the next one a click at a time; the migrated list had all 82 projects (28,000 px). Now the entries past the page size are hidden by a rule until a checkbox, the query's first child, is checked, which the author's own "Load More" button does as a `label` (one of ours, `cc-load-more`, where the page has none: previous, next and numbered pagination); no script, and one click shows everything, where the plugin loads a page at a time. The ids carry the subject (`cc-more-<template>_<queryId>`: two templates both have a query 1, and their page sizes differ). An infinite scroll is not a click, so its list stays whole (see below). `/projects/` 22.6 to 81.0.
7. **A style's `url()` in an entry is the project's own copy** (`src/emit/collections.ts`, shared `src/emit/style-urls.ts`, which `pages.ts` and `templates.ts` now use too instead of their own copies). Entries were the one emitter that did not rewrite the live address of an upload in a style: `/service/barn-painting/`, `line-painting` and `hardwood-floor-refinishing` loaded their swash backgrounds from finelinepainting.pro (`resource.live-origin`), which breaks the day the old site goes. No pixel effect.
8. **The oracle no longer calls an empty slot a broken image** (`src/verify/compare.ts`). `image.broken` (an error) counted an `<img>` with no address, which the live pages print as `src=""` and which is no failed load, and the images that are dead on the live page too (a 404 upload both sides link to). 32 errors on 16 pages, 2 left (one real: `https://finelinepainting.pro/…/iorqsmssqh0.jpg`, a 404 that the live page replaced with another image).

### What was tried and not kept

- Making `inlineGaps: "innerHTML"` the default (the space the build puts between inline siblings, `Fine Line Painting , we`): +0.06 on the 12 blog and project pages that have it. Not worth the HTML-in-attributes; it stays opt-in.
- Raising the specificity of the whole style of an entry's element (one class more) in the first version of fix 1: it beat the template's `.content-post p { margin-bottom: 0 }` (`/service/line-painting/` lost 5 points), and a rule split in two by what is contradicted keeps the template's descendant rules in their place.
- A `li > p` rule for tags no entry holds a paragraph in, and one for tables where the site has its own paragraphs: the guard is what the saved markup says (`<td><p>` exists in no post here, but the rule must not strip one that does).
- Hiding the entries past the page size of an infinite scroll too (the blog, the search page, a project type's archive): `/blog/` 61.4 to 72.4, and wrong. The live capture of `/blog/` is 5,963 px at 1366 (the scroll loaded all 11 posts) and 4,824 px at 390 (the first 5): a visitor who scrolls sees all of them at either width, so the list stays whole and the 390 capture is the live page's incomplete one. The honest number for those two pages is 65.4.

In the order of what they cost.

1. **The dump is older than the live site.** Every page ends in a footer that is not the live one (the live call to action has a photograph, a "Get Quote" button and 300 px of height at 390; the dump's has none), every service page lacks the "Simple Steps To Make Your Paint Dream A Reality" band and has a hero paragraph the live template dropped, the live single-service template names its sections `heading-cf07cdc` where the dump says `heading-c1ffff1`, the home page holds an empty three-column grid (`columns-c900eab`) that the live page no longer has and that narrows its three cards to 229 px, the related-project lists differ by two projects the dump has not (a project and a taxonomy term assigned after the export: "Log Cabin Staining Project in Fairfield", the Bethel project's `project_type`), and `/about-us/` lacks "Meet Our Team". 2. **JavaScript widgets** (accepted): the Interactive Geo Map of `/service_area/pennsylvania/` (36.5), the chat widget, the form's submission, Trustindex.
3. **Two redirects kept as pages** (`/hardwood-floor-refinishing/` 44.4, one more in `project_tag`): unchanged, `redirect.shadowed`.
